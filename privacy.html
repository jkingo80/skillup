SkillUp Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

SkillUp does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal information.

Data on your device

Your name (if you enter one), XP, skill levels, streaks and achievements are saved only on your device using standard iOS storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset it any time in Skills → Settings.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

SkillUp has no sign-in, no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

SkillUp Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information; the app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

External links

The "Find real openings" button opens a job search on Indeed in your web browser. Indeed's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

SkillUp does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
